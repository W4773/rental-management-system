# Roadmap · Alquiler Pro

[Español](ROADMAP.md) · **English** · [← README](../README.en.md)

Alquiler Pro evolves with the real needs of people who manage rentals. This roadmap is **indicative**: priorities change with usage and customer feedback.

## Vision

Anyone who owns or manages rentals should know **in seconds** who paid, who owes and how much — and be able to **collect, document and share** that information effortlessly.

## ✅ Done

**v1.0 – Foundation**
- Properties, tenants and payments (full, partial and advance) with secure multi-user (Supabase Auth + RLS).
- Gas with automatic calculation and PDF receipts.

**v1.1 – New design**
- Compact modern interface with tab navigation and logo-to-home.
- Buildings with grouped properties; editing of properties and tenants.
- Search and filters; paying several months at once; optional receipt; PDF payment reports.
- Team (owner and members) in Settings.

**v1.2 – Control and traceability**
- Month-by-month payment status (Up to date · Pending · Late) with the detail of what is owed.
- **Buildings** section, **activity log** and **active and former** tenants with history.
- Unlink a tenant without losing payments; searchable property selector.
- Annual increase with **first-increase date**.

**v1.3 – Fix and document**
- **Edit payments** and mark months as **pending** or **void** (not charged), in bulk.
- Complete documentation in Spanish and English.

## 🚧 Next

| Topic | Description |
|---|---|
| **Automatic annual increases** | Apply the increase to the rent on the configured date, with notice and log. |
| **Late-payment reminders** | WhatsApp and email reminders to tenants with pending months. |
| **Excel / CSV export** | Payments, tenants and account statements. |
| **Contracts and deposits** | Contract term, expirations and deposit tracking. |
| **Roles and permissions** | Read-only members, editors and administrators. |
| **PWA / mobile** | Installable on the phone, comfortable in the field. |
| **Charts** | Income per month, occupancy and delinquency per building. |
| **Move-in assistant** | Choose whether previous months are paid, pending or void when registering a tenant. |

- Online payments (card or transfer) with automatic reconciliation.

- Multi-currency and multi-language interface.
